Leo was a seven year old boy who dreamed of exploring the shining stars. Every night before bedtime, he looked up at the silver moon through his small telescope. One Friday evening, he built a spaceship made out of a large cardboard box, tin foil, and colorful buttons. 

He put on his shiny bicycle helmet and pressed a bright red button on his dashboard. Whoosh! In his imagination, his rocket zoomed far above the clouds. He saw purple planets, glowing comets, and friendly friendly alien creatures waving from their flying saucers. 

Leo learned that with curiosity, courage, and a good book, you can travel anywhere in the entire universe. When his mom opened the door to say goodnight, Leo smiled and whispered that tomorrow he would fly to Mars.
